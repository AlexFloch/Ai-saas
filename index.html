What it does

Writing tools built for how teams actually work

Every feature exists to remove friction between your thinking and clear, publishable writing.

Context-aware drafts

Paste your notes or link a Notion page. Synthos reads the context and writes a first draft that sounds like you wrote it — not a robot.

Shared voice library

Define your brand voice once. Every teammate writes with it. The AI adapts tone, vocabulary, and structure to match your style guide automatically.

Real-time suggestions

As you type, Synthos suggests completions and rephrases in a ghost-text layer. Accept with Tab, ignore by continuing to type.

Template engine

Build reusable templates for changelogs, onboarding emails, PRD sections, and more. Fill them with one command from any doc.

API access

Send writing tasks from your own apps via the Synthos API. Generate docs from code comments, localize content, or run batch transformations.

Privacy first

Your content is never used to train our models. Zero-data-retention mode available on all paid plans. SOC 2 Type II certified.

How it works

From messy notes to polished prose in three steps

1
Dump your raw thoughts

Paste bullet points, unstructured notes, or a rough outline. Synthos accepts anything — it doesn't need polished input to produce polished output.

2
Set the parameters

Pick your audience, tone, and format. Or let Synthos infer them from your content. Defaults are smart enough that most people never change them.

3
Refine and ship

Get a structured draft in under 2 seconds. Edit inline, ask for rewrites, or push directly to Notion, Confluence, or your CMS.
